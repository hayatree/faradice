# D3 Validation Report: capacitance sensitivities and what-if analysis (work in progress)

Design: [D3_sensitivity.md](../design/D3_sensitivity.md). All runs use the CPU engine.
Library tests: `cargo test --release -p faradice-frw --features sensitivity --test sensitivity
-- --nocapture` (S1–S3, S6, S9, S12, S14) and `cargo test --release -p faradice-sens --test whatif
-- --nocapture` (S5, S10). Long runs are `#[ignore]`d: S7 (`s7_delta_sweep`, `S7_WALKS` sets the
walks) and S13 for pillar C (`s13_whatif_sigma_coverage`).

Status: A0 (finite differences with path continuation, including faces of conductors with
conformal shells, S15/S16), C (Maxwell-matrix edits), D-del (deletion), B (potential maps,
grounded objects) and A on edge-free faces are implemented and validated. For A at edges (S8),
remedy 1 of design §6.4 is implemented and measured below; remedies 2–4 are not implemented.
D-add (additions by replay, design §8) is implemented and checked against a re-solve with the
added conductor (`cargo test --release -p faradice-frw --features sensitivity --test replay`);
no D-add runs are recorded in this report.

## A0 step bias and variance (S7)

The victim's face toward the right wire in the three-wire example (gap g = 0.14 µm, ε_r = 3.9, open
boundary with substrate), `stop_dist` pinned at 5·10⁻⁶ µm, 6.4·10⁷ walks, one set of walks for
the whole ladder δ/g = 0.25 % … 16 %. "Linear Richardson" is 2D(δ) − D(2δ). R(1/3) and
R(1/3, 2/3) are extrapolations that would cancel δ^{1/3} (and δ^{2/3}) bias terms, as a 90°
convex edge could produce. All combinations are formed per sample, so σ includes the
correlations.

dC_vv/dn (fF/µm):

| δ/g | one-sided | linear Richardson | R(1/3) | R(1/3, 2/3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 % | 8.958 ± 0.060 | 8.909 ± 0.095 | 8.770 ± 0.212 | 8.203 ± 0.593 |
| 0.50 % | 9.007 ± 0.042 | 9.032 ± 0.067 | 9.104 ± 0.150 | 8.789 ± 0.418 |
| 1.00 % | 8.982 ± 0.030 | 9.061 ± 0.047 | 9.288 ± 0.106 | 9.179 ± 0.294 |
| 2.00 % | 8.902 ± 0.021 | 9.019 ± 0.033 | 9.353 ± 0.074 | 8.671 ± 0.207 |
| 4.00 % | 8.785 ± 0.015 | 9.036 ± 0.023 | 9.753 ± 0.052 | 8.959 ± 0.144 |
| 8.00 % | 8.533 ± 0.010 | 8.971 ± 0.016 | 10.219 ± 0.036 | |
| 16.00 % | 8.095 ± 0.007 | | | |

dC_vr/dn (fF/µm):

| δ/g | one-sided | linear Richardson | R(1/3) | R(1/3, 2/3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 % | −8.118 ± 0.056 | −8.082 ± 0.091 | −7.976 ± 0.204 | −7.504 ± 0.574 |
| 0.50 % | −8.155 ± 0.040 | −8.181 ± 0.064 | −8.254 ± 0.144 | −7.923 ± 0.404 |
| 1.00 % | −8.130 ± 0.028 | −8.213 ± 0.045 | −8.448 ± 0.101 | −8.323 ± 0.285 |
| 2.00 % | −8.047 ± 0.020 | −8.171 ± 0.032 | −8.521 ± 0.071 | −7.801 ± 0.200 |
| 4.00 % | −7.924 ± 0.014 | −8.189 ± 0.022 | −8.945 ± 0.050 | −8.177 ± 0.139 |
| 8.00 % | −7.659 ± 0.010 | −8.110 ± 0.015 | −9.395 ± 0.035 | |
| 16.00 % | −7.208 ± 0.007 | | | |

Findings:

- **The one-sided bias is linear in δ**: about −0.69·(δ/g) relative, i.e. −1.4 % at 2 %, −2.7 %
  at 4 %, −5.5 % at 8 % and −10 % at 16 % of the gap.
- **Linear Richardson removes it on this face**: 9.02–9.06 from 0.5 % to 4 % (flat within
  σ ≈ 0.3–0.7 %), and about 0.6 % lower at 8 %. There is no independent δ → 0 reference here, so
  "bias" means the difference from this plateau, which is itself known only to ≈ 0.3 %. The
  R(1/3) column drifts strongly with δ, so a δ^{1/3} edge term, if present, is far below the
  linear one at these steps.
- **σ ∝ δ^{−1/2}**: σ(0.25 %)/σ(16 %) = 8.5 (√64 = 8). Richardson costs a factor 1.55 in σ.
- **Default step.** 5 % of min(extent, gap) lies between the measured 4 % and 8 % points, where
  the Richardson values differ from the plateau by 0.0 ± 0.3 % and −0.6 ± 0.3 %. The default
  is kept on that basis **for this geometry**. Other geometries (wider gaps, thick wires, corners
  of L-shapes) are not covered yet. S4 against Palace and an independent δ → 0 reference
  (pillar A) are the next checks. One-sided steps need δ ≲ 1 % for 1 % accuracy here.

A trial run at 2·10⁶ walks seemed to fit D(δ) = D₀ − k δ^{1/3}. Its smallest step was 2σ high,
and the fit did not survive the 6.4·10⁷-walk run above. Exponent fits from short runs are not
evidence.

## A at edges: hybrid with A0 on the edge strip (S8, remedy 1, partial)

`s8_hybrid_edge_strip` (`--ignored`): pillar A over the face interior (edge margin r) plus A0
on the edge strip alone. A0 moves only the strip (`FaceStep::keep` holds the interior in place),
with Richardson.

| Face | r | δ | interior A | strip A0 | hybrid | reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| free-space cube, z+ (exact C/(3a) = 0.02450) | 0.2 | 0.01 | 0.00281 ± 0.00002 | 0.02276 ± 0.00122 | 0.02557 ± 0.00122 | exact: +0.9σ; whole-face A0 0.02531 ± 0.00131 |
| | 0.1 | 0.01 | 0.00612 ± 0.00007 | 0.01851 ± 0.00110 | 0.02463 ± 0.00111 | exact: +0.1σ |
| three-wire victim, y+ (gap 0.14) | 0.04 | 0.0035 | 5.162 ± 0.009 | 3.824 ± 0.042 | 8.986 ± 0.043 | whole-face A0 (same δ) 9.073 ± 0.059 (−1.2σ); S7 plateau ≈ 9.03 |
| same, δ/r = 0.35 | 0.02 | 0.007 | 6.149 ± 0.014 | 2.694 ± 0.024 | 8.843 ± 0.028 | biased: strip at δ = 0.0035 gives 2.801 ± 0.036 |

Findings:

- **The hybrid is consistent** with the exact value and with whole-face A0 when δ/r ≲ 0.1.
- **δ must be ≪ r.** Moving only the strip leaves a step of height δ at its inner boundary. At
  δ/r = 0.35 the strip estimate is about 4 % low (−0.11 fF/µm, 2.5σ).
- **The gain is modest.** For the same A0 walks, the hybrid's variance is about 1.2–1.9× lower
  than whole-face A0. The edge strip carries 40–90 % of the derivative, and its A0 cost
  dominates. The interior A part costs well under 0.3 s. Remedies 2–4 of §6.4 remain open,
  so A0 stays the reference for faces with edges.

| a (µm) | ΔC_12 (fF) | c·φ₁φ₂ (fF) | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.4 | 0.00854 ± 0.00010 | 0.00735 | 1.16 |

  Log–log slope 0.98 between the two smallest sizes (criterion: slope within 10 %, prefactor

## A0 with conformal shells (S15, S16, design §4.4c)

`cargo test --release -p faradice-frw --features sensitivity --test shell_sens -- --nocapture`
(S15; S16 and the margin study are `--ignored`, `S16_WALKS` sets the walks per solve).

**S15, closed form.** A vertical plate (x ∈ [0.9, 1.1], domain [0, 2]³, grounded x faces,
reflecting y/z) is tagged by a conformal rule (side 0.1, top 0, ε_s). Its x+ face moves with
δ = 0.02 and Richardson. Exact, with `R = t/ε_s + (g − t)/ε_o`: `T^cond = ε₀A/(ε_s R²)`,
`T^Σ = ε₀A(1/ε_o − 1/ε_s)/R²`, and the sum `ε₀A/(ε_o R²)`. 2·10⁶ walks, 2·10⁵ shell points:

| κ = ε_o/ε_s | T^cond (fF/µm) | exact | T^Σ | exact | dC/dn | exact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.5/3.5 | 0.2406 ± 0.0059 (+0.5σ) | 0.2376 | −0.0527 ± 0.0013 (+0.1σ) | −0.0528 | 0.1880 ± 0.0060 (+0.5σ) | 0.1848 |
| 0.5 | 0.0480 ± 0.0018 (−0.6σ) | 0.0490 | 0.0468 ± 0.0014 (−1.6σ) | 0.0490 | 0.0948 ± 0.0023 (−1.4σ) | 0.0980 |
| 2 | 0.2785 ± 0.0059 (−0.8σ) | 0.2833 | −0.1384 ± 0.0034 (+1.0σ) | −0.1417 | 0.1401 ± 0.0068 (−0.2σ) | 0.1417 |
| 1 | 0.1275 ± 0.0034 (−1.1σ) | 0.1312 | 0 (no interface) | 0 | 0.1275 ± 0.0034 (−1.1σ) | 0.1312 |

The nominal C matches its closed form within 0.5σ in all four cases. The check discriminates
the medium M₁: with the nominal medium in the vacated slab (the stack inside the conductor),
T^cond would be off by a factor κ.

**S16, SKY130 met1 pair (C4 geometry, NILD3_C).** Wire a's face towards b, A0 in M₁ with
Richardson (δ = 0.012 µm) plus T^Σ (4·10⁵ points), against central differences of re-solves
at ±0.02 µm with the same stopping distance. 4·10⁶ walks per solve:

| | A0 + T^Σ (fF/µm) | of which T^Σ | re-solves | z |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dC_aa/dn | 4.862 ± 0.071 | −1.038 ± 0.009 | 4.980 ± 0.061 | −1.27 |
| dC_ab/dn | −4.517 ± 0.068 | 1.007 ± 0.009 | −4.545 ± 0.055 | +0.31 |

T^Σ is −21 % of dC_aa/dn here (the 1D gap model gave −29 %). Without it, A0 would be about
14σ off. The sensitivity run (nominal, continuations and shell term) took 54 s of wall time.

**Edge margin (S16 face, 10⁶ points).** Without the margin, points near the edges of Σ have
tiny field cubes, and single points contributed up to 10⁶ times the mean (T^Σ = −2.7·10⁵ ±
2.6·10⁵ at 1M walks). With the margin m (in units of the shell thickness, 0.030 µm):

| m | T^Σ_aa | T^Σ_ab |
|---|---|---|
| 0.25 | −1.041 ± 0.026 | 1.005 ± 0.024 |
| 0.5 | −1.026 ± 0.010 | 0.988 ± 0.009 |
| 1 (default) | −1.037 ± 0.006 | 1.006 ± 0.006 |
| 2 | −1.037 ± 0.006 | 1.013 ± 0.006 |
| 3 | −1.028 ± 0.006 | 1.013 ± 0.006 |

No trend beyond 1–2σ (≤ 1.5 % of T^Σ, ≈ 0.3 % of dC/dn), so the extrapolation bias is below
the statistical error at these sample sizes. Smaller margins only add variance.
