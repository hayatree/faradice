Design: [M2_multi_dielectric.md](../design/M2_multi_dielectric.md) (stage M2a) and
[M2b_interface_anchored.md](../design/M2b_interface_anchored.md) (stage M2b). The sections up to
L6 record the M2a runs; the [M2b section](#m2b-interface-anchored-walks) the current method. All runs use the

`cargo test --release -p faradice-frw --test stack -- --nocapture`, the long L2 run with
`--ignored l2_bias_check`; L5: seed 1), layered tables with K = 16 offsets and M = 32 cells per face
edge, half-band fractions j/32 (η_max = ½).

| cross | C(a) | 0.13783 | 0.07 % | 0.13777 | +0.04 % | +0.11 % |

Maximum error: total 0.17 %, major couplings 0.14 % (all within 1.5σ); M1 criteria (1 % / 3 %)
met with a wide margin. faradice 35 s, Palace 363 s (4 AMR meshes, last change 0.10 %).

Findings:

- **Gaussian offset.** The first run chose δ = 0.033 µm for the wires (the "δ/2 from every
  interface" rule failed by rounding at δ₀ = 0.1), which made their first hops three times
  smaller and σ 0.16 %. The offset is now chosen to maximise the smallest first cube; with
  δ = 0.1 µm the same run gives σ = 0.049 % (a: 1.09570, b: 1.09695 fF, within 0.1 % of Palace).
- **Thin layers.** The wires still stop at `max_walks` short of the `reference` target: walks in
  the 50 nm layer take cubes no larger than the layer, 57 steps per walk against 30 in the
  homogeneous V4 case. This is the expected cost of one-interface cubes (design §8) and the
  motivation for M2b (cubes containing several interfaces).

## SKY130-like stack (L6)

`bench/compare/cases/l6_sky130_stack_bus.faradice.toml`: the V8 bus (four met2 bits between three
met1 and two met3 cross lines) in a seven-zone stratified stack read off the process stack
diagram of the public SKY130 PDK documentation (Apache-2.0): FOX + PSG 3.9 up to 0.9361 µm,
LINT 7.3 (0.1 µm thick), NILD2 4.05, NILD3 4.5, NILD4 4.2, NILD5 4.1, NILD6 4.0 above 4.0211 µm.
The interfaces lie at the metal bottoms, so every conductor touches one. Conformal sidewall
dielectrics are left out (M2c). The same stack is provided as `tech/sky130_stack.tech.toml`.
Grounded box with 2 µm margin, faradice `reference` preset, seed 1, 32 threads.

Palace (order 2) stopped after three AMR meshes (last 1.85 M unknowns, cap 1.5 M); a rerun with
a 3 M cap overshot to a 5.46 M-unknown fourth mesh that was OOM-killed at 40 GB. The last change
of the diagonal was 0.72 %. The finest
mesh is therefore not converged and the geometric extrapolation of the last three meshes is the
better reference:

| | max error vs finest Palace mesh | max error vs extrapolated Palace |
|---|---|---|
| totals (9 nets) | −0.43 % (all negative, −4σ … −13σ) | 0.10 % (bus bits ≤ 0.03 %) |
| major couplings | −0.52 % | 0.27 % |

The signed pattern (all nine totals below the unconverged FEM, which converges from above)
is the same as for the homogeneous V8 case in M1. All nets converged except m3_0 and m3_1,
which stopped at `max_walks` with σ = 0.039 % against the 0.033 % target.

Cost against the homogeneous V8 run (ε = 4.0, same settings, same machine):

| | homogeneous V8 | L6 stack | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| wall time | 25.5 s | 141 s | 5.5 |
| steps per walk, bus bits | 16–18 | 23–31 | 1.5–1.7 |
| steps per walk, met1 lines | 23.5 | 87–88 | 3.7 |
| steps per walk, met3 lines | 22.3 | 37 | 1.7 |
| total steps | 5.7 G | 16.4 G | 2.9 |
| peak RSS (`/usr/bin/time`) | 6 MB | 44 MB | — |

Walks from met1 are the most expensive. met1 sits on the NILD2/NILD3 interface with the
0.34 µm NILD2 and 0.1 µm LINT zones just below; the likely cause is that cubes there are limited
by the second-nearest interface. The time per step is ≈ 1.9× the homogeneous one, presumably the
layered step itself (shrinking to a tabulated offset, 32-cell tables). Both are hypotheses until
profiled; if they hold, cubes containing several interfaces (M2b) address the step count on
real PDK stacks.

## M2b: interface-anchored walks

Walks are moved exactly onto interfaces by landing boxes, and from there take multi-layer cubes
with all interfaces at their true heights
([design](../design/M2b_interface_anchored.md)). The runs below use the code after a
performance change that made M2a steps cheaper without changing their estimator (about 1.45× on
L6): steps limited by the second-nearest interface skip the geometry query, and the layered
tables are folded into the cube's symmetry classes.

**Bias checks** (`l2_bias_check`, `l8_bias_check`, `FARADICE_BIAS_SEEDS`, 50 M walks per seed).
L8 is new: a plate whose bottom face lies on an interface, in a stack with two 50 nm layers
(`l8_thin_layers`), with an exact series-capacitor answer.

Neither version shows a significant bias at the 0.02 % level. L8 takes 9.8 steps per walk

The cost of the stack relative to the homogeneous run falls from 5.3× to 2.6× in CPU time,

### First hops next to thin layers

Half-bands of anchored cubes on lateral Gaussian faces and paired normal kernels on horizontal
faces ([design §3.5](../design/M2b_interface_anchored.md)). New exact check L9
(`l9_lateral_faces_across_thin_layers`): the L2 plate with two 50 nm high-k layers crossing its
lateral faces.

**Bias checks** (10 seeds, 50 M samples each):

| Quantity | mean error |
|---|---|
| L2 total (left / right) | +0.004 % ± 0.011 % (−0.004 % / +0.010 %) |

| L9 total (left / right) | +0.003 % ± 0.013 % (−0.016 % / +0.018 %) |

No significant bias. On L8 the σ per sample falls from 0.022 % to 0.0095 %, while the steps

**L5** (`reference`, 16 threads):

**L6**: bus and met1 results are bit-identical; their first hops do not reach a thin layer

- **met1 on L6:** 1 M walks of m1_1 take 35.4 steps per walk, down from 38.8. Landing succeeds
  69 % of the time, up from 49 %.
  - Most remaining landings start very close to an interface (`d₁ < h/16`), where no tabulated

- **L6 timing:** CPU time 2078 / 2306 s against 2456 / 2420 s without off-centre boxes, over two alternating
  pairs of runs on a loaded machine. That is about 10 % less; steps per walk are −9 % for met1,
  −3 % for the bus and −4 % for met3.
- **Bias checks** (10 seeds each, 50 M samples):

  The first ten L9 seeds gave −0.027 % ± 0.013 % (−2.2σ) and the next ten +0.021 % (+1.6σ),

## L4: parameter study

The parameters are run options (`[walk]` `interface_offsets`, `layered_resolution`,
`band_fraction`, `landing_alpha`, `antithetic`). Variants change one parameter at a time from
the defaults `K = 16`, `M = 32`, `η_max = ½`, `α = 0.7`, antithetic on.

**Bias** (`crates/faradice-frw/tests/l4_study.rs`, `bench/l4/summarize.py`):
- **Setup.** L1a, L1b, L2, L8 and L9 with exact answers; 4 seeds × 20 M samples per variant
  and case, total capacitance.
- **Result.** No variant shows a bias at the 0.05 % criterion: every per-case error is
  within 2.4σ, and every pooled error is within 0.02 %.

| Variant | Pooled bias over 5 cases | Relative variance per sample vs default |
|---|---|---|
| default | +0.010 % ± 0.009 % | 1 |
| K = 8 | +0.015 % ± 0.009 % | 0.96–1.02 |
| K = 32, M = 64 | +0.019 % ± 0.009 % | 0.98–1.00 |
| M = 64 | +0.001 % ± 0.009 % | 1.00 |
| η_max = ¼ | +0.007 % ± 0.009 % | 1.00 (L9: 1.20) |
| α = 0.5 | +0.006 % ± 0.009 % | 1.00 |
| α = 0.85 | +0.001 % ± 0.009 % | 1.00 |
| no antithetic pairs | −0.003 % ± 0.017 % | 3.2–5.1 (2.1–2.7× less efficient per CPU time) |

**Cost on real stacks** (`bench/l4/run_cli.sh`): 4 M samples per net, seed 1. CPU time is
from two repetitions, which agree within 2 %.

| Variant | L5 CPU (a, cross) | L6 CPU (m1_1, m3_0) | Steps per walk, m1_1 / m3_0 | σ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| default | 62 s | 92 s | 35.4 / 30.3 | — |
| K = 8 | 61 s | 91 s | 36.3 / 31.4 | same |
| K = 32, M = 64 | 74 s | 113 s | 34.5 / 29.4 | same |
| M = 64 | 68 s | 102 s | 35.4 / 30.3 | same |
| η_max = ¼ | 62 s | 91 s | 35.5 / 30.4 | L5 a: +12 % (variance +26 %) |
| α = 0.5 | 62 s | 92 s | 36.6 / 30.9 | same |
| α = 0.85 | 61 s | 91 s | 35.2 / 30.2 | same |

- **M.** `M = 64` brings no measurable accuracy gain and costs 10–23 % more CPU time (table
  builds and cache footprint).
- **K.** Fewer offsets take slightly more steps, and the CPU time is the same within noise.
- **α.** Insensitive between 0.5 and 0.85.
- **η_max.** Shorter bands raise the variance of lateral first hops next to thin layers.
- **Defaults kept:** `K = 16`, `M = 32`, `η_max = ½`, `α = 0.7`, antithetic on. This also
  answers design open question 1: `M = 32` tables are accurate enough.

**Aborts and stopping distance.**
- No walk hit `max_steps` in any run (0 of 112 M walks).
- On L5 (16 M samples), `walk.stop_ratio` of 3e-4, 1e-3 and 3e-3 changes wire a by at most
  0.06 % and the cross wire by at most 0.12 %. The runs decorrelate, so the σ of a difference is
  0.07 % / 0.08 % and both changes are within 1.6σ: no detectable sensitivity at this level.

**Table build cost.** The study found the fixed cost of a stack dominated by table builds:
10.5 s of single-thread CPU on L6, with 241 MB peak memory.
- Each coefficient of the double sine sums is now evaluated once instead of once per cell row,
  and the inner loops are contiguous.
- Terms below e^{−40} of the leading one are skipped.
- Builds take 1.3 s (cubes 4.0 → 0.32 s, landing boxes 4.7 → 0.66 s). A short L6 run
  (2 nets, 200 k walks) takes 4.2 s of CPU instead of 16.7 s, with 46 MB peak memory instead
  of 241 MB. Table values change only by rounding.

The C4/C5 accuracy runs above use the `reference` preset (target σ 0.033 %, at most 50 M walks
per net). With shells, both C4 wires (σ 0.038 %) and seven of the nine C5 nets (σ 0.036 %) stop
at 50 M walks; the C4 CPU times in the table come from separate runs with 4 M walks per net. One
of the 36.7 M walks of bus[2] aborted, presumably at a dielectric vertex, which has no step
(§4.6).

Where the steps go (C4, 500 k walks per net; counted with ad-hoc instrumentation not included in
the repository):

| step | share |
|---|---|
| layered axis cube | 42 % |
| stack step capped by the distance to a box | 41 % |
| unrestricted stack step | 11 % |
| homogeneous axis cube | 6 % |
| edge ball | 0.1 % |

The ratio of cube size to clearance h is spread almost evenly on a log scale from 0.5 down to
10⁻⁸: about 60 % of the steps take cubes below 0.01·h. A walk near a dielectric edge takes cubes
no larger than its distance to the edge, and in the plane across the edge that distance performs
a driftless random walk on a log scale (planar Brownian motion is neighbourhood-recurrent). The
walk therefore makes long excursions towards the edge before it either leaves or reaches the
10⁻⁸·h snap and its edge ball. Raising the snap tolerance to 10⁻⁵ and 10⁻³ (at a bias cost)
reduces the steps per walk only to 85 and 65. Time per step also rises (4.8× in C4) from the box
queries of every step near shells. The higher variance per walk is consistent with first hops
limited by the 0.03 µm strip next to the conductor sidewall.

This answers design open question 1: three-media edges make walks expensive. Making them cheaper
needs a step that crosses an edge exactly at a scale much larger than the distance to it, such as
cubes with tabulated quadrant media or a larger edge neighbourhood. That is follow-up work.

## M2d: edge cubes near dielectric edges

Design: [M2d_edge_cubes.md](../design/M2d_edge_cubes.md). Walks near a dielectric edge land on
the nearer edge plane (rotated landing boxes) and then take an edge cube centred on themselves,
2, 4 or 8 times their distance to the edge (`walk.edge_cubes`, default on). CPU engine only.

### Tests

- `edge_balls_reproduce_a_quadrant_potential` (four-quadrant product medium, exact φ) with
  walkers on the edge, a few ulps off it, on one plane near it and off both planes: within 4σ.
- `c6_potentials_near_an_edge`: three more points near the edge, within 1.5σ of the exact φ.
- `edge_cubes_agree_with_the_m2c_steps` (the C6 scene, 300 k walks): total and coupling within
  0.5σ of the M2c steps; 50.8 steps per walk instead of 159.3.
- `edge_cubes_are_taken_near_an_edge`: the step taken in all four orientations of the frame.
- Bias check (`c_bias_check`, 8 seeds): C1 −0.012 ± 0.007 %, C2 +0.004 ± 0.006 %, C1b +0.003 ± 0.009 %.

- Edge-cube tables: 18 on C4 and 36 on C5, all accepted (L1 estimates 5.6–8·10⁻⁶), about 0.3 s

  builds on C4 (2.3 s each) cost more than the steps saved.
- Coarser dielectric indices (design §3.6, after these runs): C5 with 200 k walks per net

- **The goal of design §5 is not met on C5.** Steps per walk are within 1.9× of L6 and the walks

  every step there runs several face-index queries (box distance, nearest face per axis, the
  axis-cube plan and the edge frame). On C4 the shells cost 6× (CPU) instead of 12×.

## M2e: layered first hops

Design: [M2e_layered_first_hops.md](../design/M2e_layered_first_hops.md).

- New exact case C9 (`c9_plate_between_thin_shells`, plate with 0.03 shells and slabs: series
  capacitors) at z = +1.2; without the layered hops its σ is 1.38× larger at the same walks.

  all four surfaces (the SKY130 minimum spacing in 1D), 8 seeds at σ 0.016 %: mean
  +0.005 % ± 0.006 % (+0.9σ).
- `c_bias_check` (8 seeds): C1 −0.012 ± 0.007 %, C2 −0.001 ± 0.006 %, C1b +0.006 ± 0.008 %.
- C5 at 200 k walks per net, variance relative to L6: bus 2.25–2.81 → 1.80–1.88, met1

## M2f: window first hops

Design: [M2f_window_first_hops.md](../design/M2f_window_first_hops.md).

- New case C10 (`c10_window_first_hops_agree_with_the_point_hops`): two wires with 0.03
  sidewall shells whose bottom lies on a stack plane, 200 k walks. Window hops agree with the
  point hops within 0.6σ (total, coupling), with variance 0.68×.
- Kernel tables: `tables_agree_with_a_finer_reference` checks that the true L1 error of the
  production resolution, against twice the resolution, is below 1e-4 of ‖F‖₁ (design §4.3).
- C5 at 1 M walks per net agrees with M2e on every net within 1.4σ. First-hop variance:
