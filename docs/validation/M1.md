# M1 Validation Report (work in progress)

All numbers below are reproducible with

```sh
cargo test --release -p faradice-frw --test validation -- --nocapture            # pass/fail cases
cargo test --release -p faradice-frw --test validation study -- --ignored --nocapture  # study table
```

Machine: AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ 395 (32 threads). Seed 1. Homogeneous dielectric (ε_r = 1).

## Bias study: Green-table resolution and stopping distance

`total_rel_tol = 0.1 %` at 3σ (the cube case stops at the 50 M-sample cap with σ ≈ 0.037 %).
Antithetic first hop enabled. "steps/walk" counts the transition steps of one walk of a pair.

| case | M | stop_ratio | rel. error (%) | σ (%) | samples | steps/walk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plate | 16 | 1e-3 | +0.011 | 0.033 | 20537344 | 2.5 |
| plate | 32 | 1e-3 | -0.054 | 0.033 | 20549632 | 2.5 |
| plate | 64 | 1e-3 | -0.012 | 0.033 | 20529152 | 2.5 |
| plate | 128 | 1e-3 | +0.043 | 0.033 | 20508672 | 2.5 |
| plate | 64 | 1e-2 | +0.004 | 0.033 | 20520960 | 2.5 |
| plate | 64 | 1e-4 | -0.009 | 0.033 | 20529152 | 2.5 |
| cube | 16 | 1e-3 | +0.033 | 0.037 | 50003968 | 7.7 |
| cube | 32 | 1e-3 | -0.035 | 0.037 | 50003968 | 7.7 |
| cube | 64 | 1e-3 | +0.005 | 0.037 | 50003968 | 7.7 |
| cube | 128 | 1e-3 | -0.002 | 0.037 | 50003968 | 7.7 |
| cube | 64 | 1e-2 | +0.242 | 0.037 | 50003968 | 7.3 |
| cube | 64 | 1e-4 | -0.052 | 0.037 | 50003968 | 7.8 |

Findings:

- **Table resolution**: no bias is detectable at the 0.04 % level for M ≥ 16 on these cases. The
  default M = 64 is kept; the within-cell uniform sampling is not a limiting error source here.
- **Stopping distance**: `stop_ratio = 1e-2` biases the cube by +0.24 % (≈ 6.5σ); 1e-3 and 1e-4
  are unbiased at this precision. The default 1e-3 is kept. Steps per walk grow only
  logarithmically as the ratio shrinks.
- **Antithetic first hop**: on the free-space cube the same σ needed 2.2× fewer walks than
  independent first hops (29.7 M walks → 6.7 M pairs = 13.4 M walks).

## Comparison with external field solvers (V4, V5, V8)

Run with `bench/compare/` (FasterCap 6.0.7 Galerkin BEM; Palace v0.19.0 FEM with AMR, order 2,
≤ 1.5 M dofs, 16 MPI ranks; containers capped at 40 GB). faradice uses the `reference` preset,
32 threads, seed 1, `coupling.symmetrize = "none"`. Errors are the largest over all nets; "major"
couplings are ≥ 5 % of the net total.

| Case | vs Palace, total / major coupling | vs FasterCap, total / major coupling | faradice | Palace | FasterCap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V4 three wires | 0.27 % / 0.31 % | 0.11 % / 2.2 % | 5.3 s | 105 s | 15 s |
| V5 crossing + via | 0.09 % / 0.27 % | 9.0 % / 12.7 % | 15 s | 239 s | 20 s |
| V8 SKY130 bus | 0.26 % / 0.39 % | 3.1 % / 4.0 % | 27–30 s | 511 s | 279 s |

Findings:

- **Palace agrees within the M1 criteria** (total < 1 %, major couplings < 3 %) on all cases.
  Against the geometric extrapolation of the last three AMR meshes the V4 and V5 differences fall
  to ≤ 0.1 % and ≤ 0.16 %, i.e. most of the residual is Palace discretization error (its values
  converge from above).
- **FasterCap disagrees on V5 and V8** by up to 13 %. The panelled surface area was checked
  against the structure, and Palace (an independent method) agrees with faradice, so the
  discrepancy is attributed to the FasterCap run; its cause was not investigated. On the simple V4 case it agrees except the weak 0.12 fF
  far coupling (2.2 %).

### Scaling: n-bit met2 bus

`bench/compare/scaling.py`: n met2 bits (0.14/0.14 µm, 8 µm) under two met3 and over three met1
cross lines, n + 5 nets. "1 net" extracts the middle bit only. FasterCap was given 30 min.

| bits | nets | faradice all nets | faradice 1 net | FasterCap | Palace (box) | vs Palace, total / coupling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 9 | 29.9 s | 1.0 s | 179 s (3.1 % / 4.1 % off) | 441 s | 0.28 % / 0.48 % |
| 8 | 13 | 28.0 s | 1.0 s | > 1800 s | 250 s | 0.61 % / 0.79 % |
| 16 | 21 | 32.5 s | 0.9 s | > 1800 s | 604 s | 0.67 % / 0.92 % |
| 32 | 37 | 43.4 s | 0.9 s | > 1800 s | not run | — |
| 64 | 69 | 68.5 s | 1.0 s | > 1800 s | not run | — |

Findings:

- faradice time for the full matrix grows sub-linearly with the net count (2.3× for 7.7× more
  nets); a single target net costs ≈ 1 s independently of the layout size.
- At 8 and 16 bits Palace hit the dof cap after three meshes, with a last-step change of the
  diagonal of ≈ 1.0 %, so the 0.6–0.9 % differences are within Palace's own discretization error.
- FasterCap is single-threaded here and did not finish from 8 bits on.

Caveat: wall times are for information only. The solvers run at different accuracy targets
(faradice 0.1 % / 1 % at 3σ; Palace limited by the dof cap; FasterCap `-a0.001`) and with
different parallelism (faradice 32 threads, Palace 16 ranks, FasterCap 1 thread).

## Performance notes

Single thread, 409 600 antithetic samples on `examples/three_wires.faradice.toml` (victim net),
pinned to one core:

| Change | Time | Speed-up |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline (ring search) | 0.65 s | 1.0× |
| Tighter ring stop bound (distance to the visited block) | 0.61 s | 1.07× |
| SoA per-registration coordinates (tried, reverted) | 0.78 s | 0.83× |
| **Near-cell candidate cache** | **0.43 s** | **1.5×** |

`reference` preset on the victim net (32 threads): 3.27 s → 1.09 s.
Exact nearest queries on the 1400-box bus benchmark: 2.5 ms → 0.92 ms per 10 k queries.

Cells hold only a few boxes, so vectorizing the per-cell distance loop does not pay; the cost
is in visiting cells. The cache precomputes, for every cell within two cells of a conductor,
the boxes that can be nearest to any point of that cell, so an exact query is a single list
scan. `-C target-cpu=native` gave < 5 %.
